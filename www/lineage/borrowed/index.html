Ruby's -> arrow

PHP's string increment

PHP's increment RFC, by George Peter Banyard, says in its design text that it follows Raku (PHP RFC9). It is the clearest such statement in a shipped mainstream feature.

  • The RFC surveys other languages. It describes Raku as a Perl successor whose string increment gives consistent results, and adopts that behaviour.
  • Raku had come up before: a 2013 internals thread on letter decrementing cited S03's decrement, which does not fully mirror increment (6818420).
What the sources say 1

  1. Peter Lind, PHP internals list participant PHP internals list, thread "Allow letter decrementing", message 68184 19 Jul 2013 source ↗

    Citing Perl 6's Synopsis 3 in a thread on letter decrementing, Lind notes that Perl 6 can decrement strings, though not as a full mirror image of its increment.

Swift's regex

Swift's team studied Perl 6's regexes and grammars for years, and its 2018 plan drew directly on them. The syntax Swift shipped in 2022 is more conventional, but several of the ideas arrived with it.

The 2018 plan

Michael Ilseman's January 2018 "State of String" gist set Swift the goal of beating Perl at string processing (gist10). The gist:

  • cites Apocalypse 5 as Perl's clean-up of its own regex mess, and notes that Perl 6 has a PEG-like grammar system;
  • lists Perl 6 features Swift should take outright: whitespace-insignificant syntax, named captures, multi-line matching and regex interpolation;
  • proposes ratcheting, frugal quantifiers and Raku's % separator quantifier;
  • asks whether alternation should use longest-token matching.

A5's choices from 2002 arrived two decades later, in adapted form:

  • composable, named sub-patterns, in RegexBuilder (SE-035129);
  • PEG-style ordered choice, credited to PEGs (SE-035030);
  • mandatory extended syntax in multi-line literals.

Joose

Joose is a genuine transmission from Perl 6 to JavaScript. It came by way of Perl 5's Moose, which is built on Stevan Little's prototypes of the Perl 6 meta-model (Joose manual14).
