Raku

Junctions, grammars, lazy lists, multiple dispatch, real rationals — a language with a great many ideas in it. You can run any of them right here: no install, no server, no account. 

my @names = <camelia raku moth>;
say @names.map(*.tc).join(', ');
say "and " ~ @names.elems ~ " of them";
