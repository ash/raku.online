Raku++ Internals

Film

Short animated films, each about one mechanism inside the Raku++ interpreter. Each film states a problem, draws the data structures that solve it, traces one example through them, and shows what stays outside the mechanism.

  1. 1The PadHow a variable read stopped being a hash lookup: the PadLayout, the per-frame pad, the padLive bits, and the references that know their slot.Episode 1 · narrated · Casual or Enterprise
  2. 2Die Without ThrowingHow an error reaches its CATCH as a value instead of a C++ exception: the HandedError, the handOff slot, and a default that finishes by setting a flag.Episode 2 · narrated · Casual or Enterprise
  3. 3The CarryWhat happens when an Int outgrows its 64-bit word: the overflow check, the BigInt and its base-10⁹ limbs, and the LimbVec that keeps four of them inline.Episode 3 · narrated · Casual or Enterprise
  4. 4Take, and WaitHow a gather block stops at a take and resumes later: a stack of its own, a few lines of assembly to switch to it, and a Seq that can be read only once.Episode 4 · narrated · Casual or Enterprise
Each film is drawn from the plans, commits and code in the rakupp repository, where its scenes and scripts live under docs/film. About Raku++. Pace, the project's history.
