Support

Help with Raku formulas, the add-ins that give Excel =RAKU.EVAL and Google Sheets =RAKU. How to install them and how a formula works are on the main page.

Common questions #

Excel shows #NAME?

Click the Raku button on the Home tab, once. Excel learns RAKU.EVAL when the add-in first runs. A cell that already shows #NAME? keeps it until you enter its formula again.

A cell in Sheets shows ⏳ Raku sidebar

The formula waits for the Raku sidebar, which calculates the formulas in your browser. Choose Open the Raku sidebar from the Raku menu (Extensions → Raku formulas for the add-on) and keep it open; it looks for waiting cells every second.

A formula that calls a sub from the Raku sheet shows an old result

The spreadsheet does not know that the formula depends on the Raku sheet. In Excel, press Recalculate in the Raku pane. In Sheets, the sidebar calculates the formulas again when the Raku sheet changes, if it is open; Recalculate in the sidebar does it too.

Sheets does not know RAKU in another spreadsheet

An add-on from the Google Workspace Marketplace is on in the spreadsheets you use it in. Open Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, and choose Use in this document for Raku formulas.

Where the error message is

In Excel, most errors are #VALUE!, with Raku's message under the cell's error flag; in Sheets, hover over the cell. A formula cannot read files, use the network or start programs, and its code can recurse about a hundred levels deep. A batch of formulas that runs longer than 15 seconds is stopped, and only the slow formula fails.

Reporting a problem #

Open an issue at github.com/ash/rakupp/issues, with:

  • the formula, and the values in the cells it reads;
  • what it showed, and what you expected;
  • Excel or Sheets, and where: Windows, Mac, the web, iPad;
  • the engine's version: =RAKU.EVAL("$*VM.version") in Excel, =RAKU("$*VM.version") in Sheets, and Excel's Raku pane shows it too.

For anything else, write to mail@deepsoft.online.

Removing them #

  • Excel: choose Home → Add-ins → More Add-ins → My Add-ins, then ⋯ next to Raku formulas, and Remove. An add-in you loaded from a downloaded manifest goes when you delete that file from the folder you put it in.
  • Google Sheets add-on: choose Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, then ⋮ next to Raku formulas, and Uninstall.
  • The pasted script: in Extensions → Apps Script, delete the Raku files.

Removing them leaves your formulas in place, showing an error until the add-in is back.
