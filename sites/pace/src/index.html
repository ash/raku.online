Raku++ is an independent implementation of Raku, written from nothing in C++. These chapters tell how it got to where it is. They cover the decisions that shaped its architecture, where its speed came from and where it was lost, and how its correctness was counted, including the times the count was wrong. Every date and figure comes from the release notes, plans and measurements committed in the repository.

The chapters can be read in any order. They run roughly in the order each story starts, and most of them overlap.
